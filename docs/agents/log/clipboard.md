- 2026-09-23: After #264 an OSC 52 paste into Neovim no longer loses bytes inside noctty, and #261's symptom (base64 typed into the buffer) now needs a pause inside the reply that is longer than Neovim's `'ttimeoutlen'`; the pause comes from whatever sits between noctty and Neovim. Neovim's default 50 never leaked on a local path. Over SSH with an emulated 60-100 ms round trip it leaked 13 of 13 pastes (1 KB to 200 KB) when the paste key went through `ssh.exe` and Allow on noctty's default `clipboard-read = ask` prompt was clicked 3 s later: the connection idles while the prompt waits, so TCP restarts from a small window and `ssh.exe`'s many small packets arrive in bursts one round trip apart. With `clipboard-read = allow`, or Allow within 300 ms, 0 of 17 leaked, because ssh's keystroke chaff keeps the connection busy. Trigger the paste through the terminal when measuring this: a paste started over RPC leaves the connection idle and gave misleading rates (8 of 19 even in allow mode).
